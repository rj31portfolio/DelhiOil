🏭 About Matrol®

Founded in 2000, Matrol® is the flagship brand of Delhi Oil & Chemical Company — a pioneer in India's industrial lubricant and chemical sector.

With over 25 years of leadership, we are a trusted partner to manufacturers, traders, and OEMs across India. From cutting-edge lubricants to bonding agents and specialty coatings, we deliver engineered solutions built on durability, compatibility, and cost-efficiency.
