Matrol® White Oil SN 70

MATROL® Transformer Oil premium series oils are blended from carefully selected, superior quality refined base oils and high performance additive packages consisting of active and inactive sulfur, fat and performance chemicals depending on the kind of operations . These oils are designed for lubrication and cooling of metal surfaces working under demanding metal working operations on both ferrous and non ferrous metals.

Formulated through advanced solvent refining and dewaxing processes, Matrol® SN 70 meets stringent purity standards for technical and industrial use.

⚙️ Key Features & Benefits

  • ✅Water-white clarity with low aromatic content
  • ✅Excellent thermal and oxidation stability
  • ✅Non-staining, chemically inert
  • ✅Smooth flow and easy blending with additives
  • ✅Ideal for rubber, plastics, and general-purpose formulations

🏭Applications

  • Rubber & elastomer processing (as plasticizer or extender oil)
  • Plastic molding & injection processes
  • PVC compound manufacturing
  • Industrial polishes and anti-corrosion oils
  • Printing inks and coatings
  • Textile finishing oils
  • Cosmetic or pharma intermediates (if upgraded to IP/USP spec – optional)

Property Value (Typical)
Appearance Clear, Water White
Viscosity @ 40°C ~10–13 cSt
Density @ 15°C ~0.83–0.85 g/cm³
Flash Point (COC) >160°C
Pour Point Below -3°C
