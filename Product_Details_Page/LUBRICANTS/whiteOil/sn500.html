🧪 MATROL® WHITE OIL SN 500

Heavy-Duty Process Oil for Industrial, Rubber, and Chemical Manufacturing

MATROL® WHITE OIL SN 500 is a high-viscosity, solvent-neutral base oil refined for use in demanding industrial and chemical processes. It’s ideal where higher lubrication, stability, and thermal resistance are needed, especially in rubber compounding, processing oils, and blending applications.

This grade is specially processed to be low in aromatics, clear in appearance, and chemically stable, making it safe and effective across many industrial formulations.

⚙️ Key Features & Benefits

  • ✅High viscosity and lubricity
  • ✅Excellent oxidation and heat stability
  • ✅Non-toxic, low sulfur, non-staining
  • ✅Provides body and film strength in formulations
  • ✅Suitable for both blending and direct application

🏭 Applications

  • Rubber and elastomer compounding
  • Mould release oils and hydraulic oil blending
  • Textile and leather process oils
  • Printing inks and heavy-duty lubricants
  • Adhesive and sealant manufacturing
  • Metal working and rust preventive oils
